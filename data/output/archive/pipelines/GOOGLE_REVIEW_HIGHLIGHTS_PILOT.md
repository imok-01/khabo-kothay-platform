# Google Review Highlights — Pilot Dataset

Status: FOR APPROVAL — no code, migration, or database changes made.

Observation / collection date: 2026-08-19

## Purpose

Collect a small, hand-picked set of real, verbatim Google review excerpts for 10 restaurants, to be used later (after approval) as the seed for the "Review highlights" section on restaurant pages. This pilot validates the source approach and the selection criteria before any import work.

## Rules followed

- Reviews are **verbatim** original text — typos, emojis, punctuation and Bangla characters preserved. Nothing paraphrased or rewritten.
- Reviewer attribution kept where available; otherwise flagged as unattributed.
- Each excerpt is a real review by a real customer, reproduced by a public aggregator that mirrors Google Maps reviews (Wanderlog, restaurantguru.com, top-rated.online). No scraping of Google, no AI-generated reviews.
- Selection preference: detailed reviews with specific dishes / experiences and decision-useful info; up to 3 per restaurant; a mix of positive and balanced ("X great, Y not so much") reviews; generic one-liners avoided where better options exist.
- For entries with attribution gaps (Handi, Kacchi Bhai unattributed quotes) the limitation is stated explicitly in the entry.

## Findings & post-pilot recommendation

1. **Method works.** Wanderlog reproduces Google reviews with reviewer name, date, and rating and links back to Google Maps reviews; restaurantguru.com shows recent reviews with "on Google" attribution and rating breakdowns. These are sufficient to build the pilot without scraping Google.
2. **Attribution gaps exist.** top-rated.online "highlighted reviews" text lacks reviewer names. Do not import those rows until verified against Google Maps (flagged above).
3. **Branch ambiguity is a real risk.** Pizzaburg, Handi, and Kacchi Bhai have multiple Dhaka branches; aggregator pages frequently point at the wrong branch. Always confirm the Google `placeId` for the branch matches the restaurant row before importing.
4. **Selection preference.** For most restaurants the two best picks are one strongly positive detailed review plus one balanced review. Restaurants with mostly critical reviews (Jatra Biroti, and the two Pizzaburg picks) still yielded usable balanced excerpts, but the pilot should confirm whether mixed 4/5 reviews are acceptable as "highlights."
5. **Recommended next step (pending approval):** create a `20260819000X_review_samples_seed.sql` migration inserting these rows (using the applied read-path schema), or a service-role seed script, then verify the frontend shows them.
